Add RimRunnerUtah to your Home Screen

iPhone won’t let a site install itself. Do this:

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with the arrow).
  2. Scroll and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.

It shows up like an app. Open it from there instead of Safari.
